## 2. Guidelines for Forks and Modified Distributions

You are free and encouraged under Apache 2.0 to fork the code, modify it, experiment, and distribute your modified version. However, to prevent user confusion and preserve software provenance:

### Requirements for Community Forks:
1. **Distinct Project Name**: You must use a completely distinct and unique name for your fork (e.g., *OpenPalette*, *WinLaunch*, *DeskCommand*).
2. **Distinct App Icon**: You must replace the official AltSpace icon and branding with your own visual assets.
3. **Accurate Origin Attribution**: You may state that your project is based on AltSpace in documentation or "About" dialogs using factual phrasing:
   - ✅ *Allowed*: "OpenPalette — Built on the open-source AltSpace core."
   - ✅ *Allowed*: "A community launcher forked from the AltSpace codebase."
4. **Prohibited Branding**:
   - ❌ *Not Allowed*: Calling your fork "AltSpace", "AltSpace Community Edition", "AltSpace Pro", or "AltSpace Fork".
   - ❌ *Not Allowed*: Using the official AltSpace logo, icon, or trademarked branding in your application window, installer, or website.
   - ❌ *Not Allowed*: Implying endorsement, sponsorship, or affiliation with the official AltSpace project or maintainer.

---

## 3. Official Releases and Hosted Services

Only releases cryptographically signed with the official project Authenticode certificate and distributed via `https://github.com/codecheesee/altspace` or the official Windows Package Manager (WinGet) manifest are official AltSpace builds.

Forks and modified versions may not connect to official AltSpace hosted cloud services, hosted AI routing endpoints, or commercial entitlement backends without express authorization.
