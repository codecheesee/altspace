Frequently Asked Questions

How does AltSpace recognize that I'm subscribed to Pro?

When you sponsor via GitHub Sponsors, our automated GitHub webhook logs your GitHub handle to the verified manifest. When you open the AltSpace desktop app and click "Sign in with GitHub", it matches your authenticated handle and unlocks Pro in under 3 seconds.

Can I use AltSpace completely free for commercial work?

Yes! AltSpace is released under the permissive Apache 2.0 Open Source License. You can use it freely for personal, freelance, startup, and enterprise engineering workflows.

Does AltSpace send my file names, clipboard, or keystrokes to the cloud?

Never. AltSpace is strictly local-first. Searches run directly against your local NTFS Everything journal, clipboard history stays in DPAPI-encrypted RAM, and local AI uses your own Ollama engine. We do not operate any tracking, metrics, or telemetry servers.
